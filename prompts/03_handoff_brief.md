# Prompt: broker handoff brief

**Input:** lead profile, seminar, discovery answers, full interaction timeline, appointment.

**Output (in English, with key Chinese terms kept where the lead used them):**
- **Who:** name, language preference, seminar, source
- **Need:** goal, timeline, budget, area, first-time / pre-approved
- **Context:** 1–2 lines from their own words
- **Suggested talking points:** 3 bullets
- **Open questions / risks:** what is still unknown

Max 120 words. Factual only; mark anything inferred as "(inferred)".
